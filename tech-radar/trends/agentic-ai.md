Systems where LLMs plan, call tools, and take multi-step actions toward a goal
with limited human intervention. The shift from single-shot prompting to
goal-directed agents is one of the defining directions of the year.

## Why this ring

Real value is emerging in narrow, well-scoped workflows, but reliability,
cost, and guardrails still demand careful human oversight. Trial on tasks that
can tolerate supervision and clear rollback.

## Notes

Watch evaluation and observability tooling — agent debugging is still immature.
