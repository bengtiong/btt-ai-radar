Frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex and similar) that abstract prompting,
retrieval, tool calling, and agent loops.

## Why this ring

Useful for prototyping but abstractions move fast and can obscure behaviour.
Assess carefully; many teams outgrow them and drop to thinner libraries.
