Stores optimised for similarity search over embeddings, underpinning most RAG
and semantic-search systems.

## Why this ring

Mature and widely supported, with both dedicated engines and vector features
added to mainstream databases. Adopt where you need semantic retrieval.

## Notes

For smaller corpora, a vector extension on your existing database is often
enough — don't add a new system reflexively.
